Online Student Leave Application System

A comprehensive digital administrative portal streamlining student leave submissions and institutional faculty approvals.

Problem Solved:

Traditional paper-based leave slips in colleges cause physical bottlenecks, delayed teacher signatures, misplaced records, and zero transparency. This project digitizes the entire lifecycle—allowing students to submit digital leave requests with categorized justifications and giving faculty a centralized dashboard for real-time approvals.
